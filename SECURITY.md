# Security Reporting

Do not publish vulnerabilities, credentials, client data, or exploit details in public issues.

**Private reporting channel:** TBD — configure a monitored company email or private security reporting process before wider distribution.

When reporting, include affected system, impact, reproduction steps, and relevant timestamps, but do not attach real secrets or unnecessary personal data.

For suspected active compromise: stop risky activity, preserve relevant logs, notify the designated security owner through the approved private channel, and follow `docs/security/incident-response.md`.

This file is a starter policy. Confirm ownership and the private reporting channel before considering it operational.
