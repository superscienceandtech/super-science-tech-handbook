# Client Lifecycle

**Status:** Proposed workflow — adapt to actual contracts and team capacity.

1. **Inquiry:** Capture source, contact consent, business need, urgency, and owner.
2. **Qualification:** Confirm fit, budget range if voluntarily supplied, decision process, constraints, and risks.
3. **Discovery:** Document user needs, current systems, success measures, and assumptions.
4. **Proposal:** Define scope, deliverables, exclusions, milestones, responsibilities, fees, and change control.
5. **Contract and kickoff:** Confirm signed agreement, IP/data terms, access, communication, and acceptance process.
6. **Delivery:** Track tasks, demos, risks, decisions, and changes in the approved project system.
7. **Acceptance and handover:** Record acceptance, documentation, deployment details, and training.
8. **Support / closure:** Confirm support terms, access removal, retention obligations, and lessons learned.

The company CRM project has previously been described as a flow involving `ProjectInquiry → Lead → Company → Contact → Activity`; verify the current implementation before treating it as deployed.
