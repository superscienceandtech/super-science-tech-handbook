## Approval rules (proposed)
- Informational project documentation: project owner review.
- Engineering standards: engineering owner approval.
- Security policies: designated security owner and leadership approval.
- HR, legal, financial, or contractual policies: appropriate authorized owner and qualified adviser where needed.
- Company strategy: founder / designated leadership approval.

Until roles and approvals are confirmed, documents remain Draft or Proposed.
