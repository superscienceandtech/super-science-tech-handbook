# AI Research Lifecycle

**Status:** Proposed process.

1. Research question and motivation
2. Literature and prior-art review
3. Hypothesis, baseline, and evaluation plan
4. Data provenance, permissions, privacy, and ethics review
5. Experiment design and versioned configuration
6. Reproducible runs, metrics, and error analysis
7. Results with limitations and negative findings
8. Independent review where feasible
9. Decision: continue, publish, prototype, productize, or stop
10. Archive artifacts and record dependencies needed to reproduce the work

For each study, record dataset version and rights, model/checkpoint, code commit, compute environment, random seeds where relevant, metrics, limitations, and conflicts of interest. Research owner, publication approval, and budget process: TBD.
