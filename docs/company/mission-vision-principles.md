# Mission, Vision & Operating Principles

**Status:** Proposed — requires founder approval.

## Mission (proposed)

Build useful, reliable technology through client-focused engineering, company-owned software products, and rigorous AI research.

## Vision (proposed)

Create a sustainable technology company whose client work, products, and research reinforce one another and deliver measurable value.

## Operating principles (proposed)

1. **Customer value:** Understand the problem before choosing technology.
2. **Evidence over claims:** Measure outcomes and distinguish results from hypotheses.
3. **Engineering quality:** Prefer secure, tested, maintainable systems.
4. **Research integrity:** Make experiments reproducible and report limitations.
5. **Ownership:** Every deliverable has a responsible owner and clear acceptance criteria.
6. **Documentation:** Record decisions and operational knowledge so work is not trapped in one person's head.
7. **Security and privacy:** Minimize access and data collection; protect client and company information.
8. **Sustainable execution:** Set realistic scope, communicate risk early, and learn from incidents.
