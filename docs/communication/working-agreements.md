# Communication and Working Agreements

**Status:** Proposed.

- Record decisions in a durable place rather than relying only on chat.
- Every task should have an owner, expected outcome, and due date when applicable.
- Raise blockers early with context and a proposed next step.
- Keep client and internal discussions in approved channels.
- Share status in the team's agreed format and cadence.
- Respect focus time and time-zone differences.
- Meetings should have a purpose, owner, and action items.

Official tools, channels, meeting cadence, response-time expectations, and working hours: TBD.
