# Incident Response

**Status:** Proposed starter procedure; confirm owners and contact paths.

1. **Detect and report:** Use the approved private channel. Do not post secrets publicly.
2. **Triage:** Identify affected systems, data, accounts, timeline, and potential impact.
3. **Contain:** Revoke exposed credentials, isolate affected resources, and restrict access as appropriate.
4. **Preserve evidence:** Retain relevant logs and timestamps without unnecessary copying of sensitive data.
5. **Recover:** Restore trusted service, rotate credentials, patch root cause, and validate operation.
6. **Communicate:** Notify designated leadership, affected parties, and authorities as required by contract and applicable law.
7. **Learn:** Record root cause, impact, corrective actions, owners, and due dates.

Incident commander, escalation contacts, legal/privacy reviewer, and notification obligations: TBD. For an active incident, use the verified internal escalation process rather than relying on this draft alone.
