# Team Conduct

## Our standard

We aim to maintain a professional, inclusive, respectful, and safe environment for employees, contractors, collaborators, and clients.

Expected behaviour:
- Treat people respectfully and critique ideas, not individuals.
- Communicate clearly and assume good intent while addressing harmful impact.
- Protect confidential information and respect client agreements.
- Give useful, specific feedback.
- Raise security, safety, harassment, or misconduct concerns through a designated channel.

Unacceptable behaviour includes harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate disclosure of confidential information, and retaliation against people raising concerns.
