## Working agreements

The handbook’s [contribution guide](CONTRIBUTING.md) asks contributors to make focused changes, label unverified details `TBD` and recommendations `Proposed`, and request review from a relevant owner. The [documentation standards](docs/governance/documentation-standards.md) ask authors to state purpose, scope, status, ownership, and review context; distinguish facts from assumptions; and keep confidential data and secrets out of the repository.

Team practices in [working agreements](docs/communication/working-agreements.md) and the research process in [research lifecycle](docs/research/research-lifecycle.md) are marked **Proposed**. Follow their source pages for status and detail; do not infer approval from their presence here.

## Document status

- **Draft** — initial content that has not been approved.
- **Proposed** — suggested guidance or process awaiting a decision.
- **Approved** — explicitly approved by the authorized owner.
- **Archived** — retained for history and no longer current.

Where a page does not clearly state its status or owner, treat it as unconfirmed and consult [document control](docs/governance/document-control.md) or [open questions](docs/governance/open-questions.md). The handbook itself is an initial draft; role assignments, approvals, and review cadence may still be TBD or proposed.

The handbook currently lists its repository owner and policy approver as TBD, with a quarterly review cadence marked Proposed. See [document control](docs/governance/document-control.md) for the source and approval notes.
