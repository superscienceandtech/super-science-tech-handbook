## Business pillars

1. **Client services:** Build and maintain custom software for clients.
2. **Own software products:** Develop and operate products owned by the company.
3. **AI research:** Investigate, evaluate, and prototype AI/ML ideas, with a path from research to reproducible results and products.

These are the confirmed strategic pillars for this handbook. Specific offerings, product names, team assignments, pricing, revenue targets, and research commitments remain **TBD until confirmed**.

## How to use this handbook

- Read `docs/governance/documentation-standards.md` before editing.
- Use a Pull Request for material changes.
- Assign a real owner and review date before a document becomes an approved policy.
- Mark uncertain details `TBD` or `Proposed`; never invent facts.
- Keep secrets, passwords, private keys, client confidential data, and sensitive employee records out of this repository.
- Store project-specific implementation details in the relevant project repository and link to them here where appropriate.

## Repository status labels

- **Draft:** Initial content; not yet approved.
- **Proposed:** Suggested approach awaiting decision.
- **Approved:** Explicitly approved by the authorized owner.
- **Archived:** Retained for historical context; no longer current.
